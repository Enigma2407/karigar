Understanding
the need.

Our study focused on understanding artisans, their work and the need for better digital visibility.

OUR OBJECTIVES
01

Understand

To understand the types of products created, the materials used and the general processes followed during production.

02

Document

To examine how product photographs, descriptions, categories and contact details could help customers understand the products being offered.

03

Identify

To identify the information that could be presented through a website for artisans.

04

Develop

To gain practical experience in field research, observation, communication, documentation and requirement analysis.
